    Accept payments

    Collect money from a customer.

    Checkout

    Take a card or bank payment on a Paga-hosted page. The customer is redirected to Paga, pays, and Paga notifies your server.

    Dynamic virtual account, Bank USSD, Paga wallet and USD bank transfer

    Collect money from a customer with a payment request. The same request serves the virtual account, USSD, wallet and USD flows — you pick the method.

    Refunds

    Refund a payment in full or in part, or cancel one that was never paid.

    Direct Debit (Open Banking)

    Take a standing authorisation to debit a customer's bank account, then charge against it.
